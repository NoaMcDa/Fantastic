Color

Dark-mode first, clinical rather than wellness-green. Surfaces step in lightness and never in hue; one gold accent carries every action; the verdict colours belong to the Keto Lens judgement and to nothing else.

One accent, one meaning: gold marks the single primary action on a screen and the user's own progress. It is never used to signal that food is good or bad — that is the verdict palette's job.
