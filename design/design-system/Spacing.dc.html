Touch targets & RTL
44 × 44 minimum. A 20px icon keeps its 44pt hit box through invisible padding — the visual size and the tappable size are separate decisions.
Mirror every directional glyph. In RTL a “forward” chevron points left. Progress fills, swipe-to-delete and sparklines all flip with it; clocks, cameras and the flame do not.
