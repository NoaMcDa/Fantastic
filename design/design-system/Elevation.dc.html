Fantastic · Design System · 04

Elevation

On a dark interface a shadow alone is nearly invisible. Height is read first from the surface getting lighter, second from a hairline, and only then from the shadow underneath.
Levels
elevation/0 — flush
no shadow · 1px hairline rgba(235,235,245,0.10)
Sections that sit on the background: grouped settings, empty states
elevation/1 — card
0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.32) · surface/raised
Every card and list row on the dashboard and diary
elevation/2 — sheet
0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.44) · surface/overlay
Bottom sheets, the Keto Lens results panel, pickers
elevation/3 — dialog
0 16px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.56) · surface/overlay
Alerts and confirmations, always over a scrim
elevation/glow — accent
0 8px 24px rgba(245,166,35,0.28)
The FAB and the streak ring only — the two things that should feel lit from inside
Focus & hairlines
focus/ring
1px #F5A623 + 0 0 0 3px rgba(245,166,35,0.30)
Keyboard and VoiceOver focus, and the active text field. Never removed — only restyled.
hairline/soft · 10% — inside a card
hairline/base · 16% — card and input outline
hairline/strong · 24% — selected outline
Hairlines render at 1 physical pixel. On a dark surface they read as light, not as a border colour — hence the alpha, never a flat hex.
Scrim
scrim/modal
rgba(0,0,0,0.60) · fade 200ms ease-out
Behind every sheet and dialog. Tapping it dismisses anything non-destructive; a confirmation that would lose data ignores it.
