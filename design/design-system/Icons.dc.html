Fantastic · Design System · 05

Iconography

One stroke family, drawn on a 24pt grid. Outlines everywhere; a solid fill is a state, not a style — it marks the selected tab and an active streak, and nothing else.
Construction
24 × 24 canvas, 20 × 20 live area — the dashed box. Nothing crosses it.
Stroke 1.75, round caps, round joins, no scaling of stroke with size.
Inner corners never sharper than 2. Diagonals at 45° where the subject allows.
Colour is inherited — every icon paints in currentColor.
