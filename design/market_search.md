# Market Research — Competitor Analysis

## Target Market

- **Primary:** Israeli keto practitioners (Hebrew speakers)
- **Secondary:** General keto dieters on iOS
- **Niche:** Beginners navigating the adaptation phase + experienced ketonians managing ratios and biomarkers

---

## Competitor Landscape

### 1. Keto Chef (כיתו שף)
**Platform:** iOS / Android
**Focus:** Israeli keto recipes and meal planning

| Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|
| Israeli recipes with local ingredients | No macro tracking |
| Hebrew-language UI | No label scanner |
| Culturally relevant content | No adaptation phase guidance |
| Kosher-filter option | No biomarker/symptom diary |
| Recipe costing in NIS | No restaurant directory |
| Strong Israeli community following | Limited personalisation |

**How to beat them:** Fantastic adds every tracking feature they lack while keeping the Israeli cultural context (Hebrew labels, local restaurants, local ingredient names). We don't just give recipes — we tell users *why* their body is reacting, what phase they're in, and whether the product they're holding is actually keto.

---

### 2. Carb Manager
**Platform:** iOS / Android / Web
**Focus:** Carb and macro tracking, keto-specific

| Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|
| Massive food database | No Hebrew OCR |
| Barcode scanner | Israeli food products rarely in DB |
| Net carb calculator | No adaptation phase tracking |
| Premium meal plans | Expensive premium tier ($60/yr) |
| Widget support | No electrolyte advisor |
| Good charts and insights | No symptom/biomarker diary |

**How to beat them:** On-device Hebrew OCR removes the biggest friction point for Israeli users — products simply aren't in Carb Manager's database. Fantastic works offline on local labels with zero wait time.

---

### 3. Cronometer
**Platform:** iOS / Android / Web
**Focus:** Micronutrient and biomarker tracking

| Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|
| Deep micronutrient breakdown | Not keto-specific |
| Blood biomarker logging | No OCR |
| Detailed reports | Complex UI, steep learning curve |
| Free tier is generous | No adaptation phase concept |
| Trusted by health professionals | No restaurant analysis |

**How to beat them:** Cronometer is for nutritionists. Fantastic is for someone who just started keto and needs hand-holding through the first 28 days — plain language, phase-aware advice, not scientific reports.

---

### 4. MyFitnessPal
**Platform:** iOS / Android / Web
**Focus:** General calorie and macro tracking

| Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|
| Largest food database (~14M items) | Calorie-first, not keto-first |
| Social features and community | Net carbs calculation is clunky |
| Barcode scanner | No Hebrew label support |
| Recipe importer | No adaptation tracking |
| Integration with Apple Health | Heavy, slow app |

**How to beat them:** MFP tries to serve everyone; Fantastic serves keto practitioners specifically. Keto ratio, electrolyte targets, and phase-aware guidance are built into the core, not bolted on.

---

### 5. KetoDiet App
**Platform:** iOS / Android
**Focus:** Keto recipes and basic macro tracking

| Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|
| Clean keto-only food database | No Hebrew or Israeli support |
| Recipes with keto ratios | No OCR or label scanner |
| Meal planning | No biomarker logging |
| Offline-first | No adaptation phase tracking |
| One-time purchase model | Limited to English recipes |

**How to beat them:** Localization. Their content is English-only and their food database has zero Israeli products. Fantastic is built from the ground up for the Israeli market.

---

### 6. Zero (Fasting App)
**Platform:** iOS / Android
**Focus:** Intermittent fasting, often combined with keto

| Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|
| Beautiful, simple UI | Fasting-only, no keto tracking |
| Streak gamification done well | No food or label analysis |
| Science-backed coaching | No electrolytes |
| Strong retention via habit loops | No local relevance |

**How to beat them:** Not a direct competitor, but their streak UI and habit-loop gamification is the gold standard — Fantastic should match that quality for the adaptation phase tracker.

## Opportunities & Gaps

1. **No app does Hebrew OCR** — the entire Israeli market scans labels by eye. First-mover advantage is wide open.
2. **Keto adaptation is under-served** — most apps log data but don't *explain* what's happening to the user's body day-by-day.
3. **Israeli restaurant scene is uncovered** — keto-friendly restaurants in Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, Haifa have no dedicated index.
4. **Electrolyte awareness is low** — keto flu is the #1 reason beginners quit. An app that specifically coaches Na/K/Mg targeting the adaptation phase would reduce churn for the whole category.
5. **Monetisation gap** — Carb Manager charges $60/yr for features Fantastic will include in the base tier. A ₪39–59/month premium model with a free core is likely the right pricing for the Israeli market.

---

## Risks

- **Carb Manager could add Hebrew OCR** — they have the engineering resources. First-mover window is roughly 12–18 months.
- **Keto trend cycles** — Israeli keto adoption peaked ~2021 and is stable. The market exists but is not growing explosively.
- **Apple App Store competition** — Carb Manager and MFP dominate search rankings. ASO (App Store Optimisation) in Hebrew is critical from day one.
